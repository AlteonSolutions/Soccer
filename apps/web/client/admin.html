One player per line: the name, then their parents' emails.

Parents sign up by picking their player from this list, in the roster's order. The player's emails get the confirmation, the Monday snack reminder and the Thursday game reminder. Emails are never shown outside this page.

    Site Settings

    The team name is the heading on the sign-up page and the emails. The coach email is where {{coach}} goes in the email templates. The allergies become a sentence above the games ("peanut and tree nut food allergies"); leave the field empty for "no food allergies".

    Team Badge

    Any image file up to 2 MB. It appears in the header of both pages and as the browser tab icon.

    Snack Duty Logo

    Optional. Shown centred at the bottom of the sign-up page, under the games. A transparent PNG looks best.

    Email Templates

    Four emails go out: a confirmation right after a sign-up, the Monday reminder to the family on snacks, the Thursday reminder to every family, and a Monday nudge to you when a game has no sign-up. Words in double braces, like {{team}}, are filled in when the email is sent. The To and BCC lines take {{parents}}, {{team_parents}}, {{coach}} and plain addresses, separated by commas; put the team in BCC so families never see each other's addresses.
